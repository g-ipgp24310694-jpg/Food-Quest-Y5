📄 View lesson transcript
  1. Food gives us energy to learn, play and grow. We can make thoughtful choices!
  2. Everyday foods include fruit, vegetables, grains and protein foods. Try a variety.
  3. Foods with lots of added sugar or salt are often better as sometimes treats.
  4. For a balanced plate, try about half fruit and vegetables, a quarter grains or starchy food, and a quarter protein. Choose plain water.
💚 There are no bad people or bad meals. We are learning how to build balanced habits.
